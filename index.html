Security & data residency

Built to pass your client's security review.

You're handling other people's drawings. Here's exactly what happens to them — written to be pasted straight into the IT-approval email.

In-network AI
Extraction runs on AI models hosted inside our own cloud account. Your documents are never sent to a third-party AI vendor, and they are never used to train models.
Purged within 72 hours
Uploaded PDFs are deleted from our storage within 72 hours of processing, with a hard 30-day backstop. We keep the extracted fields — never your drawings.
US and Canadian data planes
Your account is pinned to a region. Documents, OCR, and the document database stay in-region — United States or Canada, your call at signup.
Non-destructive by design
Nectar renames in place and exports copies. It never deletes files from your disk and never rewrites a drawing's contents. Installers are signed and notarized, with cryptographically verified auto-updates.

FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the beta request and we'll answer directly.

What does Nectar cost during the beta?

Nothing. Beta licenses are free and come with a credit grant — one credit covers one file, end to end. We'll work out fair pricing with beta users before launch.

What happens when it reads a sheet wrong?

You fix it in seconds — the sheet and its fields sit side by side, and clicking any OCR line drops that text straight into the field. Report a bad extraction and the credit is refunded automatically.

What kinds of sets does it handle?

The messy real-world ones: large-format scans, as-builts, sheets that are rotated, skewed, or hand-stamped, and non-standard title blocks. That's the corpus it's built and tested against.

What happens to my files?

On your machine: originals are renamed in place, exports are separate copies, and nothing is ever deleted. In our cloud: processing copies are purged within 72 hours.

Which platforms does it run on?

Native desktop apps for Windows (x64) and macOS (universal). Installers are signed and notarized, and updates install themselves silently after cryptographic verification.

How does licensing work?

Per-seat license keys, one machine per seat. You manage seats yourself in the customer console — add one, release one, or move a seat to a new computer without emailing anyone.

Can our data stay in Canada?

Yes. Canadian accounts run on a dedicated Canadian data plane — documents, OCR, and the document database stay in Canada. Ask about residency in your beta request and we'll set your account up on the right plane.
